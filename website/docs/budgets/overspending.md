# Overspending

A category is **overspent** when you spend more than its balance. Its Balance turns into a red, negative amount. Actuality gives you two ways to deal with it next month, chosen per category.

## Choose how overspending is handled

Select the red balance to open **Overspending Settings**, and choose:

- **Subtract it from next month's "Available to Budget"** (the default). Next month, the category starts again from zero, and the overspent amount appears in next month's header as **Overspent in** this month, reducing Available to Budget.
- **Subtract it from next month's category balance**. The negative balance stays in the category and carries into next month, shown with a small red arrow. You cover it by budgeting more to that category.

Select **Done** to apply it. The setting applies to this month and every later month, replacing any setting those months had. Earlier months aren't changed.

:::note The same setting in Actual

In Actual's web app, the second option is called **Rollover overspending**. Actual's guides that tell you to turn on "Rollover overspending", such as its guide to carrying credit card debt, mean the second option here.

:::

Actual only stores this setting for about a year ahead. Actuality extends it automatically as new months are added, so a category you set up today keeps the setting.

## Fixing overspending this month

You can cover overspending before the month ends:

- **Budget more** to the overspent category, if Available to Budget has money left.
- **Move money** from a category that has some to spare: lower that category's budgeted amount, then raise the overspent one's by the same amount.
- Select the overspent cell, press **F2** and choose **Balance to 0.00** to budget exactly enough to bring it back to zero.

## Overspending and credit cards

Spending on a credit card works like spending from any other budget account: the purchase comes out of the category you assign it to. If a category is overspent by a credit card purchase, the overspending is handled in the same way as above.
