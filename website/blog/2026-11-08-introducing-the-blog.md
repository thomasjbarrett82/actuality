Actuality now has a blog. It's where each new version is announced, with a short summary of what changed and the full list of changes, and where other news about Actuality is posted.

<!-- truncate -->

## Keeping up to date

- **Read it here.** New posts appear at the top of the [blog](/blog). The [Release](/blog/tags/release) tag lists every version's release notes.
- **Subscribe to the feed.** Add [the RSS feed](https://actualitydesktop.org/blog/rss.xml) to your feed reader to hear about each new post.

The [User Guide](/docs/intro) is still the place to learn how Actuality works. Release posts link to the guide pages that describe what changed.
