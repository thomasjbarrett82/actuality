Actuality 0.2.1 makes date fields and the calculator easier to use, and the Windows downloads smaller. Download it for Windows, macOS or Linux from the [releases page](https://github.com/thomasjbarrett82/actuality/releases).

- **A calendar in every date field.** It opens when you move into the field. Select a day, or ignore it and keep typing: **Tab** closes it and moves on, and **Esc** closes it and undoes what you typed. See [Entering transactions](/docs/accounts-and-transactions/entering-transactions).
- **The calculator closes the way you'd expect.** Selecting anywhere else, or switching to another app, closes it everywhere in Actuality and keeps the amount you had before you opened it.
- **Reconciling** starts when you press **Enter** in the dialog, and an invalid statement date shows **Invalid date** instead of going ahead.
- **Smaller Windows installers.** There's now a separate installer for x64 and for arm64, so you only download the one your computer needs.
